1 Aegis IAM

Aegis IAM is the corporate identity and access platform for employees, customers, applications, and auditors. This book demonstrates engineering as code: the canonical graph preserves needs, requirements, architecture decisions, architecture, implementation, tests, and evidence in one verifiable chain. The showcase is published as a Quarto Book (Posit PBC 2026), so requirements engineering artifacts and conventional scholarly citations coexist in the same source tree.
