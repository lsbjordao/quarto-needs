4  Functional requirements

Functional requirements detail authentication, authorization, lifecycle, audit, and recovery behavior.

4.1 FUN-001 · Validate corporate credentials

functional-requirement approved high

The service shall validate credentials against the corporate directory before creating a session.

4.1.1 Rationale

Ensures the session represents a known corporate identity.

Need relations
Derives from
SYS-001 — Authenticate identities before issuing a session
Implemented by
COMP-001 — Authentication service
Verified by
TC-001 — Authentication with a valid credential

4.2 FUN-002 · Require MFA for privileged access

functional-requirement approved high

The service shall require a second factor for privileged roles.

4.2.1 Rationale

Reduces the effectiveness of credentials captured through phishing.

SYS-001 — Authenticate identities before issuing a session

4.3 FUN-003 · Revoke sessions after a risk change

functional-requirement approved high

The service shall revoke sessions when the account is suspended or risk increases.

4.3.1 Rationale

4.4 FUN-004 · Evaluate attribute-based policies

functional-requirement approved high

4.4.1 Rationale
