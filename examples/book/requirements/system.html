3  System requirements

The system catalog connects business expectations to the Aegis IAM architecture.

3.1 SYS-001 · Authenticate identities before issuing a session

system-requirement approved high

Aegis IAM shall authenticate the identity before issuing a usable session.

3.1.1 Rationale

Prevents protected resources from being exposed to anonymous requesters.

3.3 SYS-003 · Manage the account lifecycle

system-requirement approved high

The system shall provision, modify, suspend, and terminate accounts in an auditable manner.

3.3.1 Rationale

Orphan accounts and persistent privileges are significant operational risks.

3.5 SYS-005 · Recover access with identity proofing

system-requirement approved high
